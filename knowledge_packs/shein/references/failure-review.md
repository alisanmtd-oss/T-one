## Review loop

1. Capture the observed result and safe trace before changing the hypothesis.
2. Classify whether the cause is evidence, scope, authorization, data quality, policy, connector or external action gating.
3. Add one minimal preventive rule and regression case; avoid broad permanent rules from a single event.
4. Re-run the affected regression case and the store-identity/external-effect suites.
5. Promote the learning only after the new evidence status and validity are recorded.
