## Decision rules

- Call a feature `platform_native_ai` only when an official source explicitly identifies it as AI and the seller-facing or internal boundary is recorded.
- Call automated recommendations `native_automation` when official text proves automation but not AI. Listing Optimizer currently stays in this category.
- Before using a seller-facing capability, prove the exact store, site, mode, ownership, execution identity and permission. Otherwise return `available_unconnected`.
- Do not convert internal monitoring, image recognition or risk detection into a seller control surface.
- Do not infer paid advertising from promotions, traffic opportunities, solution-provider listings or a marketing tab. Paid SHEIN ads remain `research_only` until a store-specific surface, billing identity, permission and reporting source are verified.
- Any experiment must record the original and variant listing, eligible SKU/site, start/end time, exposure, performance metric, approval, outcome and rollback. A public A/B feature description alone is not a completed experiment.
- A native-AI training increment requires an actually opened tool surface and recorded input/output boundary. A login redirect or generic public page is evidence of a permission boundary, not tool competence.
- Do not interpret an `intelligent tools` developer-account category as platform-native AI availability. Require a named tool, invitation/permission, opened surface and observed input/output.
- Keep Seller Hub Analytics and Listing Optimizer as separate capabilities. Public sales-trend graphs do not prove optimizer inputs, recommendations, experiment attribution or a writeback path.
- Treat unlabeled webinar/interview statements as speaker-ambiguous `single_case` evidence. Mentions of AI, chatbots, structured data or organic visibility are not platform-native AI or ranking rules until the named SHEIN surface and current official/store-scoped behavior are observed.
- Treat comment learning as a separate evidence track. Reaction counts and login gates never substitute for visible comment bodies, sort coverage, author replies or counterexamples; when access is blocked, produce no comment-derived AI or operating rule.
- A first-party video title/description is not tool competence. When captions are unavailable, playback is incomplete or comments are zero, retain only the observable metadata and record `no_increment_from_content` rather than reconstructing the presentation.
