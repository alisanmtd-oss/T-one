## Time-sensitive snapshots

- US Pro Seller page last updated 2026-07-16: Pro tier currently requires OTD ≥95%, cancellation ≤1.5%, 48-hour response ≥95%, free ThreeDay-or-faster coverage ≥50%, content ≥75%, price competitiveness ≥75%, more than 100 completed orders in rolling 90 days, at least 90 active days and policy compliance. Metrics are calculated daily; status refreshes on the 5th and 20th. Recheck before operational use.
- CA Pro Seller page last updated 2026-06-08: OTD ≥90%, actual versus promised delivery ≤3 days, cancellation ≤2.5%, 48-hour response ≥80%, content ≥70%, price competitiveness ≥70%, 25 completed orders in rolling 90 days, at least 90 active days and policy compliance. Recheck instead of copying US thresholds.
- The Pro Seller Insights API guide establishes that MX has only the Pro-seller level, but current first-party MX thresholds were not established. Keep thresholds `unknown` and never copy US/CA values.
- Marketplace SEM public documentation captured 2026-07-18 shows US-only availability, a $5–$2,500 daily budget range, possible daily spend up to 2× budget, a 30.4× monthly cap and a 14-day sales attribution window. These are especially time-sensitive; recheck before any campaign or financial decision.
- The SEM campaign-recommendations guide had a cURL/Python request-field conflict. Treat the connector schema as blocked until API Reference or an authorized sandbox validates it.
- On 2026-07-18 the US item-spec table showed `5.0.20260501-19_21_29-api` as recommended for `MP_ITEM`, `MP_MAINTENANCE`, `MP_WFS_ITEM` and `OMNI_WFS`. This is a dated snapshot, not a hard-coded default; check the table and diff report before every release or feed migration.
- The public US Get Spec reference showed a limit of 20 Product Types per request and a maximum of 3 transactions per minute per seller. A real call still requires a matching OAuth token and store scope.
- CA and MX pages establish site-specific taxonomy/spec behavior. Their public visibility does not authorize Seller Center or API access, and US endpoints/versions must not be silently reused.
- US Seller Performance Standards page last updated 2026-04-29 and US seller-fulfilled returns page last updated 2026-05-14. Recheck thresholds, windows, address rules and exemptions before enforcement.
- WFS fees, size/weight limits, product restrictions, promotions, carrier programs, fulfillment-center coverage and API schemas are time-sensitive by country. Never freeze them into the stable Skill.
- US WFS recommendation documentation captured 2026-07-18 exposes four distinct read surfaces: inbound quantity, restock, convert-to-WFS candidates and unpublished-item insights. All outputs are recommendations or estimates and require eligibility, margin, inventory and owner gates before a write.

Exact CA/MX guide routes displayed localized `No Data found`; keep availability `unknown`, not unavailable.
