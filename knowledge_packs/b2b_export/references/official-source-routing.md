|---|---|---|---|---|
| Incoterms | https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/incoterms-2020/ | Global B2B goods contracts | ICC owns the current 11-rule Incoterms® 2020 standard; specify rule, exact named place, and version | Does not set payment, title transfer, product compliance, or customs classification; use official text/training for interpretation |
| Export documents | https://www.trade.gov/common-export-documents | U.S. exporter guidance; useful document taxonomy for global B2B | PI is a negotiating/quotation document; commercial invoice, packing list and transport documents have distinct roles | Destination and product can require additional documents; confirm with importer, forwarder, customs and origin authority |
| Payment methods | https://www.trade.gov/report/trade-finance-guide | U.S. exporter guidance; global payment risk concepts | Compare cash-in-advance, LC, documentary collection, open account and other methods by buyer/seller risk | Not bank approval, legal advice, credit decision, or proof of payment |
| Restricted-party screening | https://www.trade.gov/consolidated-screening-list | U.S. controls; parties to relevant exports/reexports/transfers | CSL is an aid; potential matches require due diligence and official-list checks | Match is not an automatic legal conclusion; origin, jurisdiction, item, end use and parties matter |
| U.S. sanctions search | https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-list-search-tool | U.S. sanctions nexus | Screen names and retain query inputs/results before commitment when applicable | Fuzzy result needs human review; do not clear a deal from a no-match alone |
| U.S. export controls | https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/732 | Items/activities subject to U.S. EAR | Check denied parties, end users, end uses, destination and license requirements | Do not assume Chinese-origin apparel/equipment is or is not subject to EAR without a jurisdiction analysis |
| China export controls | https://exportcontrol.mofcom.gov.cn/article/zcfg/gnzcfg/flfg/202111/226.html | Exports from China; controlled items and related data/services | Require item, end-user/end-use and control-list review before equipment commitment | Do not infer license status from a product name; route controlled-item questions to qualified compliance owner |
