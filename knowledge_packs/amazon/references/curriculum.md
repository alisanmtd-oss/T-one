# Independent Amazon curriculum

The machine-readable source of truth is `config/amazon_training_curriculum.json`.

Cover all domains before treating the agent as a complete Amazon operator:

1. Account registration, verification and selling plans.
2. Account health, policy and appeals.
3. Product research, category and demand.
4. Unit economics, cash flow and break-even.
5. Catalog identity, GTIN, brand and required attributes.
6. Variations, bulk files and batch repair.
7. Listing SEO, keywords and copy.
8. Images, video, A+ and brand content.
9. Pricing, Featured Offer and buyability.
10. FBA/FBM fulfillment and delivery promise.
11. Inventory, forecasting, aged and unfulfillable stock.
12. Orders, customer service, returns, refunds and claims.
13. Reviews, voice of customer and product-quality feedback.
14. Sponsored Products.
15. Sponsored Brands, display and video advertising.
16. Ads measurement, attribution and experiments.
17. Coupons, deals, promotions and stacking.
18. Brand Registry and intellectual-property protection.
19. Business reports, Brand Analytics and growth review.
20. SP-API, Ads API, roles, reports, finances and restricted data.

Graduate a domain only after it has current official evidence, verified store/software evidence, an independent market or customer signal, a counterexample and passing eval cases. Legacy recovered chats never satisfy the official-evidence layer.
