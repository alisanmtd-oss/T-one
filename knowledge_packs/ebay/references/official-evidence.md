Treat `EBAY_MOTORS_US` as a category-specialized route with its own compatibility, condition and listing metadata; never inherit an apparel schema. Keep `EBAY_RU` unexecutable until the main integration performs a current legal, availability and risk review.

## Seller-experience handling

Seller forums, communities, videos and tool claims may suggest hypotheses about title structure, auction timing, Best Offer, price endings, promoted ad rates, shipping, watchers or conversion. Record each as `seller_hypothesis` with source/date/sample/site. Do not convert it to an official rule; require an approved, store-scoped experiment with a primary metric, secondary metrics, stop rule and result writeback.
