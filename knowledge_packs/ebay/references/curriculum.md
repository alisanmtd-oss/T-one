## Module completion levels

- `L0_unknown`: no current external evidence.
- `L1_public_verified`: first-party public page/software observed.
- `L2_sandbox_verified`: legal sandbox input/output observed.
- `L3_store_read_verified`: bound-store read and scope observed.
- `L4_write_gated_verified`: confirmed write executed and persistence verified.

The current eBay expert may claim no level above `L1_public_verified` for seller-scoped eBay capabilities.

Every module regression must include at least one anonymous non-private_tenant product. The evaluation set covers antique furniture, beauty, electronics, digital goods, collectibles, home decor, musical instruments, games, lighting, coins, kitchen appliances, watches, garden products and books. Passing these cases proves only scope behavior in training assets; it does not prove a seller connection or live business completion.
