## Evidence policy

- Official agreements and current authorized-store responses outrank ERP documentation and seller anecdotes.
- A seller anecdote may become a dated experiment hypothesis, never a platform rule.
- A public URL or tool label proves discoverability only. It does not prove OAuth, store access, ads access, write scope, or an isolated execution identity.
- Store facts must include capture time and `store_binding_id`; credentials remain only as `credential_ref`.
- A public community report about affiliate/developer onboarding is a dated `single_case` only. It may reveal a test to run, but cannot establish identity federation, credential compatibility, portal routing, or official support behavior.
- Hosting on an AliExpress media/CDN domain is not enough to prove official authorship, current applicability, ownership, or reuse rights. An unattributed public Seller Workbench PDF observed on 2026-07-19 contained store-specific examples; its operational details were excluded and its provenance remains `unknown`.
