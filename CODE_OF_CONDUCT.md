# Community Conduct

T One welcomes operators, developers, creators, researchers, and platform specialists from different markets and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence without exposing private business or personal data.
- Correct platform taxonomy and policy claims with primary sources where possible.
- Respect consent, intellectual property, platform rules, and local law.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing credentials, private messages, customer data, precise private locations, or account identifiers.
- Advice intended to bypass identity checks, account controls, platform enforcement, payment safeguards, or legal restrictions.
- Spam, deceptive outreach, fake engagement, review manipulation, or unauthorized automation.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious safety or privacy matters should be reported privately through GitHub's security reporting channel.
